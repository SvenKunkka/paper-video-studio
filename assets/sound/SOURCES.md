# Sound sources

All recordings in this folder come from Wikimedia Commons and are marked **public domain**, converted to 48 kHz mono FLAC.

| File | Used for |
|---|---|
| Turning_a_page.flac | ribbon flops in the cascade (the swish, then the pat on touchdown); the slap under the final click |
| Rejection_letter_cut_into_pieces.flac | real paper snips in the rip and in the shredder bite |
| Book_paper_pages_assorted.flac | paper sliding in the rebuild, and the feed before the bite |
| Shredder_hum.flac | the motor bed in the shredder shot, retimed to follow the load drop and the spin-down |
| Hardcover_books_dropped.flac | low weight under the final click |

Everything else is synthesised in `scripts/sound.py`: tear crackle, whooshes, flutter, motor harmonics, the cutting
grain, strip clicks and room tone.
